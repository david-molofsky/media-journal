Media Journal

Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Media Journal ("the app") is a personal media-tracking application. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and how it's stored.

Overview

Media Journal is offline-first: your journal entries, wishlist, ratings, notes, tags, custom media types, podcast subscriptions and preferences are stored locally on your device using your browser's built-in storage. Media Journal does not require an account and does not operate a central library database. Data leaves your device only when you choose to use an external lookup, import, connection, export or backup feature, as described below.

Google Drive access

Media Journal offers an optional backup feature that uses the Google Drive API to save a copy of your library to your own Google Drive account.

  • What we access: Media Journal requests the drive.file scope, a restricted, per-file permission. The app can only see and manage files it itself creates in your Drive — specifically, a folder named "Media Journal" containing your backup files. It has no access to any other files, folders, or content in your Google Drive.
  • What we don't access: Media Journal cannot read, modify, or delete any other files in your Drive, and has no access to your Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or any other Google service.
  • What's stored in the backup: Version 2 backup files contain journal entries, media-type definitions, podcast subscriptions and safe preferences. Authentication tokens, server credentials and device-specific settings are excluded.
  • Where your Google credentials go: Authentication is handled entirely by Google's own sign-in flow (Google Identity Services). Media Journal never sees or stores your Google password. The app stores only a short-lived access token, kept locally on your device, used solely to make Drive API requests on your behalf.
  • Third parties: Media Journal does not sell your Google Drive data or use it for advertising. Library contents stored in a Drive backup are not sent to analytics services.

Your control over this data

  • Connecting Google Drive backup is entirely optional; the app is fully functional without it.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive access at any time from within the app's settings. This removes the locally stored access token.
  • You can revoke Media Journal's access at any time directly from your Google Account permissions page.
  • You can delete backup files at any time from within your own Google Drive, in the "Media Journal" folder.

Other data the app accesses

When you request metadata, cover art, barcode information or podcast discovery, Media Journal may send a title, creator, ISBN, UPC, service identifier, podcast search term or feed URL to the relevant provider:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB)
  • ComicVine
  • Google Books
  • Google image search
  • Open Library and Open Library Covers
  • UPCitemdb and UPCMDB
  • Apple Podcasts search and the publisher hosting a selected RSS feed

Some of these requests pass through Media Journal's API proxy so browser-based requests can reach the provider. The proxy receives the request data and normal network information such as the device's IP address. Lookup results are used to populate entries or subscriptions in your library.

Connected import services

You can optionally connect Media Journal to services that hold your media history. The app requests only the information needed to show an import preview and create the entries you select.

  • MyAnimeList: uses MyAnimeList's sign-in and API to retrieve selected anime and manga list information.
  • Trakt: uses Trakt's sign-in and API to retrieve watch history, ratings and watchlist information.
  • Audiobookshelf: connects to the server address you provide to retrieve audiobook library and progress information.
  • Jellyfin: connects to the server address you provide to retrieve played media information.
  • Plex: connects to the server address you provide to retrieve watched movie and television information.

Connection tokens, server addresses, user identifiers and related settings are stored locally on your device. Passwords entered to connect to Audiobookshelf or Jellyfin are used for the connection request and are not included in Media Journal backups. Tokens and server credentials are excluded from exported backups. Requests to a self-hosted service are also subject to that server operator's logging and privacy practices.

Manual imports and exports

Files you select for import are processed on your device. Media Journal shows a review or restore preview before applying supported imports. Files you export are saved to the location you choose and remain under your control.

Data retention

Local library data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear the browser's site data. Connected-service credentials remain locally until you disconnect the service or clear site data. Google Drive backup files remain in your Drive until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when Media Journal's data access or use changes. Where required, you will be asked to review or consent to a change before continuing to use the affected feature.
